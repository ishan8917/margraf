Torino Outlet Village

Versatility, durability, and elegance within contemporary architectural spaces.

With over 29,000 square meters of Santafiora®, Torino Outlet Village stands as a concrete and significant example of how our material adapts seamlessly to every type of cladding and to large-scale architectural contexts. The versatility of Santafiora® emerges throughout the entire project, where floors, walls, staircases, and bathrooms are unified by a coherent and functional material choice.

The natural stone integrates harmoniously within the spaces, helping to define a refined and measured aesthetic capable of balancing technical requirements, durability, and aesthetic value. The unique characteristics of marble make it an ideal choice for outdoor architecture: its resistance to atmospheric agents ensures high performance over time, without compromising the elegance of surfaces exposed to sun, rain, or wind.

The final result stands out for the understated elegance of our exclusive natural stone, enhanced by an architectural project that is attentive to detail and material quality. The extensive use of the material is never redundant; instead, it reinforces the identity of the place, making it distinctive and coherent.

Designed by architect Claudio Silvestrin, the Torino Outlet Village project demonstrates how a conscious choice of materials can profoundly influence the quality of spatial experience. Santafiora® once again confirms its versatility and durability, establishing itself as the perfect choice for large surfaces and outdoor spaces, without sacrificing an essential and contemporary aesthetic.
