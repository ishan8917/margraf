Back
Color: White
Material: Marble
Type: Natural Stone

Calacatta Cremo is a high-end marble featuring an elegant ivory-white background, enriched with wide grey-beige veining and fine copper-beige lines that add depth and sophistication to any surface. Ideal for luxury interior design applications, this material is perfect for bathrooms, kitchens, flooring, wall cladding, and other refined furnishing solutions.

Available finishes: polished, honed.
