The Company

We have been doing what we were born to do, for more than 100 years. We extract and process marble and other natural stones from all over the world, using our experience to make them even more perfect.

We turn nature into architecture, because this is what we do best: we supply architects and designers with the raw material to construct living spaces of authentic beauty.

The story of Margraf – originally Industria Marmi Vicentini – began in 1906, in Chiampo, a small village in the province of Vicenza.

About us

Marble and natural stone processing

From extraction to processing into unfinished blocks and slabs, from the most sophisticated processes to marble tiles and custom-made products: when people think marble, they think Margraf. And not just in Italy.

Marble, Margraf. Thanks to our deep connection with the material, our strong focus on research and the artisan savoir faire that characterises every stage of the production process, we are an international benchmark for the processing of marble and natural stone.
